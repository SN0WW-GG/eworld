Shipping & Buyer Protection

Payments

All transactions are processed through encrypted Stripe Checkout links. EWorld does not store payment card information.

Shipping

Sellers must ship orders within 3 business days and provide a valid tracking number. Seller payouts are held until tracking is confirmed.

Disputes & Refunds

If an item does not arrive or differs significantly from the listing description, buyers must report the issue with photos within 48 hours of delivery for a full refund.
